# Child Safety & Privacy Policy

This is the most important document in this repository. A childcare center's
reputation — and the safety of its families — depends on getting this right.
Everything the automation does is built around these rules.

## The core rule

> **Bright Stars never publishes identifiable photos or names of enrolled
> children without a signed photo-release on file for that specific child, and
> never reveals anything that could expose a child's location or schedule.
> When in doubt, leave the child out.**

Some families have custody disputes, safety concerns, or restraining orders. A
single photo can put a child at risk. This is not optional or "best effort."

## How the automation protects you

1. **Faces-out by default.** The AI is instructed to write image briefs that use
   classrooms, materials, children's art (no faces), teachers, illustrations, or
   stock imagery — never identifiable children.
2. **Human approval gate.** Nothing publishes automatically. Every post is drafted,
   then a person reviews and approves it before it can go live.
3. **Consent flag on every post.** Each generated post has a `notes` line telling
   the reviewer exactly what to check (e.g. "Get written consent from the staff
   member featured" or "No consent needed").

## Your checklist before approving any post

- [ ] Does this post show or name an identifiable child? → **Only proceed if a
      signed release is on file for that child.** Otherwise swap to a no-faces visual.
- [ ] Does it feature a staff member or a parent? → **Get their written consent first.**
- [ ] Does it reveal a schedule, room location, or anything identifying a family? → **Remove it.**
- [ ] Are you reposting a parent's review/testimonial? → **Get permission to share it.**

## Photo-release records

Keep a simple, current list of which children and staff have signed releases
(a spreadsheet is fine). Before using any real photo, check the list. If you're
not 100% sure, use an illustration or stock image instead — the AI always
provides a safe alternative image idea.

## If something is posted by mistake

1. Delete the post from the platform immediately.
2. If it involved a child without consent, notify the family.
3. Note what happened and how it slipped through, and adjust the process.
